# Multi-Asset Risk–Return Analysis
# ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

THREAD: I analyzed 8 assets vs. S&P 500 over the 2022–2025 rate-hike cycle.
Here's what the data shows. 

1/ Gold (GLD) outperformed every equity in the universe.
   ~167% cumulative return. Sharpe > 1.35.
   R² vs S&P 500: 0.009.
   Near-zero equity correlation = real diversification.

2/ Tesla had the highest beta (2.05) AND the worst risk-adjusted return.
   Sharpe: 0.14. Max drawdown: -71.7%.
   High beta ≠ high return. Risk amplification without fundamental support.

3/ JPMorgan was the best equity.
   144% cumulative return. Positive Jensen's Alpha (+0.116).
   The rate hike cycle directly expanded its net interest margins.

4/ AAPL, SPY, QQQ correlation: 0.87–0.99.
   If you own all three, you essentially own one position.
   "Diversification" across equities isn't diversification.

5/ CVaR > VaR for every asset (as expected).
   But the gap is widest for CL=F (crude oil): the tail losses
   are 40% worse than the headline VaR figure.
   VaR alone understates oil risk significantly.
